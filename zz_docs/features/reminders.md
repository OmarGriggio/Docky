# Payment reminders

Reminder levels, their settings, sending and printing them.

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-26 - keep it up to date in the same commit as the code it describes.

## Backend

- **Payment reminders** (since 2026-11-24, `modules/reminders`, `033_add_reminder_settings.sql`/`034_add_template_reminder_texts.sql`/`035_add_invoice_reminders.sql`): three levels - 1st reminder, 2nd reminder, formal notice ("mise en demeure") - set by the company's admin on **Paramètres > Rappels** (`reminder_settings`, one JSONB row per company, `GET`/`PUT /reminder/settings`; no row: `DEFAULT_REMINDER_SETTINGS`, 10/14/14 days, no fee): each level on or off (the 1st always on), its delay **after the previous reminder was sent** (the 1st's after the invoice's due date) and its fee in CHF. `reminder.rules.ts` (pure, tested: `normalizeReminderSettings`, `nextReminderOf` - the first enabled level past the highest sent, its due day, its fee and the fees so far - `isDueBy`). **A reminder is recorded only when sent** ("Envoyer le 2e rappel", `POST /reminder/document/:id`, `sendReminderServ`): the level is never taken from the request, only for a SENT invoice past its due date with something left to pay (deposits deducted), once that level is due - 409 `REMINDER_INVOICE_NOT_SENT`/`_NOT_OVERDUE`/`_NONE_LEFT`/`_NOT_DUE` otherwise (a double click never sends two levels) - under the invoice's lock (`FOR UPDATE`, plus `UNIQUE (document_id, level)`). `invoice_reminders` keeps what it went out with: its date, fee and `total_fees`, the invoice's `amount_due` then, its level's **text** (the template's `reminder_text`/`_2`/`_3` - `reminderTextOf(template, language, level)`, a blank one that level's default in the client's language, `template.defaults.ts`) and the client's address then (`buildBilledAddress`). Only the latest of an invoice can be taken back (`DELETE /reminder/:id`, 409 `REMINDER_NOT_LATEST`). `GET /pdf/reminder/:id?level=n` prints a sent one as it went out, without `level` the next one previewed (today, its template's text, the client's current address) - `reminderToPrintServ` gives `ReminderPdfGenerator` its `ReminderToPrint`. The PDF is titled per level ("2E RAPPEL - FACTURE N° ...", "MISE EN DEMEURE - ..."), lists the invoice's balance, the fees and the total under its text when it has fees, and its QR-bill asks for the balance plus the fees - never the invoice's own total. `GET /reminder/to-send` (dashboard + invoices READ): the invoices whose next reminder is due (`getInvoicesToRemindServ`); `GET /document` carries each one's `reminder_level` (the highest sent). Invoices READ to see them, WRITE to send or take back. The template preview takes a `level` too. **Frontend**: `features/settings/reminders` (Paramètres > Rappels, read only for anyone but the admin), `features/reminders/invoice-reminders`'s `InvoiceRemindersDialog` (the reminders sent - each PDF, the latest taken back - and the next one: its due day and fee, "Aperçu", "Envoyer le ..." confirmed then its PDF opened), opened from the documents list's ⋮ ("Rappels", an overdue invoice or one with reminders - its "Rappel 2" tag), a sent invoice's form and the dashboard's **"Factures à relancer"** card (only while there's one); the template editor's Rappel tab has one text per level (`reminderLevel`, previewed). Tested: `reminder.rules.test.ts`, `dto/reminder.dto.test.ts`, `reminders.test.ts`. The weekly demo reset deletes company 2's settings (its reminders go with its documents).
