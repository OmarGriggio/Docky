# Templates ("Modèles")

A quote's, an invoice's and a reminder's look, default texts and conditions - several per company, frozen on a document once sent.

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-26 - keep it up to date in the same commit as the code it describes.

## Backend

- **Templates ("Modèles")** (since 2026-11-19, `025_add_templates.sql`/`026_add_document_template.sql`/`027_drop_old_document_settings.sql`, `modules/templates`, Paramètres > Modèles): everything a quote's, an invoice's and a reminder's look and default texts come from, in one place, **several per company, exactly one its default** (`templates_one_default`, a partial unique index - `setDefaultTemplateInDB` unsets the old one first, in a transaction, the index being checked row by row), archivable (not the default - a 409). A template holds: the **look** - `layout` CLASSIC/MODERN/BANNER, `font`, a **palette** of up to 5 named colors (`palette`, JSONB `[{ name, color }]`) and each PDF element's color in it (`element_colors`, JSONB `{ element: position from 1 }` - `COLOR_ELEMENTS`: text, title, companyName, sectionTitles, tableHeader, total, earlyPayment, conditionsHeading, scaleEarly, scaleNet, approval, footer; left out: none) since 2026-11-20 (`028_add_template_palette.sql` turned the two colors into a palette of two and the elements they colored, sent documents' frozen look included; `029_drop_template_two_colors.sql` dropped them), `show_currency` ("Afficher la monnaie", `030_add_template_show_currency.sql`, off by default: "CHF" before the amount on the total's line and a final invoice's balance only - the PDFs' lines, section totals and other amounts never print a currency; every line table, classic details page and modern table, has a Type column, Matériel/Service in the client's language), `header_image` (its own upload route); the **texts** a new document starts from - `quote_`/`invoice_introduction`/`_conclusion`, `quote_`/`invoice_due_days`, and `reminder_text`/`reminder_text_2`/`reminder_text_3` (one per reminder level - copied onto a reminder when it's sent; blank: that level's default text in the client's language, `reminderTextOf`, see [reminders.md](reminders.md)); the **conditions** - `payment_terms` and an invoice's early payment discount. Checked by `template.rules.ts`'s `normalizeTemplateData` (pure, tested: a name, known layout/font else the defaults, a palette of `#rrggbb` colors named 30 characters at most ("Couleur n" when blank) and element positions within it (`normalizePalette`/`normalizeElementColors` - anything else dropped), texts 10'000 characters at most, due days, the discount rules the profile had). The company profile keeps only its identity (name, address, VAT, IBAN/QR-IBAN, reference type, footer details, logo) - the migration copied each company's look, texts and conditions into one **"Standard"** default template, then dropped those columns and `document_templates`; a company gets its Standard template with its creation (`addCompanyServ`) - one without any (made in SQL) gets it the first time it's asked for (`getDefaultTemplateServ`, a unique violation from a concurrent creation read back). `GET /template` (`?includeArchived=true`), `GET /:id` - quotes or invoices READ; `POST /`, `POST /:id/duplicate` `{ name }` (header image shared), `PUT /:id` (a field left out kept), `PATCH /:id/default|archive|unarchive`, `POST|DELETE /:id/header-image` - ADMIN/PLATFORM_ADMIN only, every id the company's own. **A document has a template** (`documents.template_id`, null only for a chantier's PROJECT document): `POST /document`'s `template_id` (the company's own, active - a 404 otherwise; the default when left out), `PUT /document/:id` changes it (left out: kept) - a draft STANDARD invoice then takes the new template's early payment discount (`earlyPaymentOf`; a deposit never has one); a deposit invoice takes its quote's template (the default for a chantier made by hand). **Its look is frozen once sent** (`documents.template_style`, `{ layout, font, palette, element_colors, show_currency, header_image }` - a malformed palette read as none, `documentStyleOf`): taken from its template inside the UPDATE that leaves DRAFT or accepts (`document.repository.ts`'s `frozenStyleOf`), cleared when a quote goes back to DRAFT, taken afresh when a SENT quote is edited - like `billed_address`; never from a request; the migration froze every document already sent with its company's look. The PDFs print `documentStyleOf(template_style, template)` (`PdfSource.style`, the font, colors, layout and header image - `DocumentPdfGenerator.load`). A quote has no payment terms of its own: its template's, frozen in `template_style` with its look (`payment_terms`, `quotePaymentTermsOf`, tested - `032_freeze_sent_quote_payment_terms.sql` froze the quotes already sent with their template's current ones), its `{{conditions_paiement}}` too; documents frozen before `show_currency` existed were given it on (`031_show_currency_on_sent_documents.sql`) so they keep "CHF" on their total. "Facturer" / "Facture finale" start from the chantier's quote's template (`GET /project`'s `quote_template_id`, `document-form.ts`'s `applyFromProject`), like its deposits. **CLASSIC** is `business-letter.ts` (the layout above), **MODERN** `pdf/templates/modern-letter.ts` (logo top left and the company's details top right without a header image, the address on the left with date/client n°/references/due date in a right-hand column, the title large in the secondary color, every line of every section on page 1 in one table - its header row in the main color, `accent-color.ts`'s `textColorOn` picking white or black text, repeated on each page it runs onto (`PdfWriter.pageCount`), rows shaded every other one, each section closed by its total - no details page, the totals in a framed box with the VAT number under it); **BANNER** `pdf/templates/banner-letter.ts` (since 2026-11-26, `037_add_banner_layout.sql` lets it past `templates_layout_check`): a band of the title's color (dark gray without one) across the top of page 1 - the logo on a white tile and the company's name, address, phone and email on the left, the document's kind ("FACTURE", "2E RAPPEL", "SECOND REMINDER") large and its number under it ("N° FAC-...", a reminder's "Facture N° FAC-...") on the right - passed apart by each document, never cut out of a title - at most 42 % of the band's width, shrunk to fit down to 12 pt, the company's name shrunk down to 10 pt before anything is cut (text black or white, `textColorOn`); with a header image, the band sits under it between the margins with the kind and number only - the client's address on the right (a Swiss envelope's right window), every detail of the document in a framed three-column grid (date, client n°, reference, due date or a quote's validity, service period, location - a long value takes two or three cells, then wraps - never cut: a sent document has nowhere else to show it), each section in a table of its own (a bar of the sections' titles color before "Pos n – title" - a long title wraps, its column titles on a light tint of the table header's color, repeated on each page it runs onto, a subtotal) - no details page - and the modern layout's totals box (`drawModernTotals`) with the payment conditions on its left (`conditionsBesideTotals`, a `BesideTotals` the box keeps on its page - the payment terms and an invoice's payment scale, not the due date or a quote's validity, already in the grid: room otherwise empty, and what keeps them off the QR-bill's page; past 220 pt they go under the conclusion instead, `drawConditionsBlock`, where they can run onto the next page), then the conclusion (and a quote's "Bon pour accord", tighter than in the other layouts); its footer on a light band of the footer's color with a stronger rule on top (`drawBannerFooterBand`, from `drawLetterFooters` - only when the footer has something to say). `layoutOf` (template.types.ts) reads an unknown layout - stored or frozen - as CLASSIC. Invoice, quote and reminder templates branch on `letter.company.layout`. Fonts **HELVETICA** (standard) / **LATO** / **PT_SERIF** (`pdf/core/pdf-fonts.ts`: free OFL fonts in `backend/assets/fonts`, embedded subset with `@pdf-lib/fontkit`, read once and cached; the prod image copies `assets/` - `backend/Dockerfile`), passed to `PdfWriter.create({ font })`; the QR-bill's page switches back to Helvetica (`useStandardFont`/`useDocumentFont` in `qr-bill-page.ts`). **Frontend**: `features/settings/templates` - `TemplateList` (`/settings/templates`: colors swatches, default badge, edit/make default/duplicate/archive for the admin, "Afficher les archivés") and `TemplateEditor` (`/settings/templates/:id`: the name; Apparence - `shared/components/document-style-picker`'s layout and font cards, the palette (add up to 5, rename, remove - the elements' positions follow, a text's `[cN]` tags don't), each element's color (a select of the palette, "Aucune"/"Noir") grouped by zone of the page in reading order - header, line table, totals, conditions, details pages, footer, text - with a line saying where it prints, the zones, their order and the elements following the layout picked: only those it prints (`color-elements.ts`'s `colorZonesOf`/`elementHintKey`, tested - classic has no table header nor company name, banner no company name; a hidden element keeps its stored color), each with a "Repérer" eye: the preview printed again with that element alone in a vivid pink (`POST /template/:id/preview`'s `highlight`, `template.rules.ts`'s `withHighlight`, tested - a sixth palette color for the preview only, never saved), on a document that prints it (`previewKindFor`: "Bon pour accord" on a quote, the early payment line and scale on an invoice - said to need a discount when the template has none - a reminder only its letterhead, text and footer), the header image; Textes - Offre/Facture/Rappel tabs with the text style toolbar and the placeholders; Conditions - payment terms and the early payment discount; read only for anyone but the admin; beside it (below it under `xl`) a **live preview** - `POST /template/:id/preview` `{ type: QUOTE|INVOICE|REMINDER, template: the unsaved fields }` (quotes or invoices READ, nothing written: `previewTemplateServ` checks them like a save over the stored template, header image the stored one's) prints `template.sample.ts`'s `sampleSourceOf` (pure, tested: a made-up client, two sections, in the user's language, the template's texts/due days/discount, the company's real identity and logo) through `DocumentPdfGenerator.generateFrom` - a QR-bill the profile can't make yet is left out (`PdfSource.sample`), refreshed 600 ms after the last change, following the texts' tab, an iframe (a link to the PDF on a phone); the route's `wide` data lets `settings.ts` drop its 900px column for it), `TemplateService`. The quote/invoice form's "Modèle : X" button (a step of its guided tour) picks the document's template among the active ones (`document-form.ts`'s `pickTemplate`: the introduction, conclusion and payment terms still as the previous template wrote them follow the new one, the due date while it's the default one), a new document starts from the default one (`loadTemplate`), a duplicate from its source's; the text blocks' "Sauvegarder" (an admin's) writes the draft into that template. The profile says where all this went. The weekly demo reset recreates company 2's one Standard template and freezes its sent documents. Tested: `templates.test.ts` (defaults, rules, duplicate, documents given one, discount on change, frozen look, the preview, tenants, roles), `document-style.test.ts` (a quote printed in all nine layout/font combinations, an invoice and reminders in the banner layout), `template.rules.test.ts`.
