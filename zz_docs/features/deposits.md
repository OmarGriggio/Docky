# Deposit invoices and the final invoice

Factures d'acompte, the chantier's billing card, and the final invoice deducting them.

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-26 - keep it up to date in the same commit as the code it describes.

## Backend

- **Deposit invoices ("factures d'acompte")**, since 2026-11-13 (`documents.invoice_kind` `STANDARD`/`DEPOSIT`, `017_add_invoice_kind.sql`, never taken from a request): `POST /project/:id/deposit-invoice` `{ percent }` (of the accepted quote's total incl. VAT - a 400 `DEPOSIT_NEEDS_QUOTE` for a chantier created by hand) or `{ amount_incl_vat }`, at any time - in progress or completed - until the chantier's final invoice exists (a 409 then), not on an archived chantier. `document.service.ts`'s `addDepositInvoiceServ` creates an ordinary DRAFT invoice (FAC- numbering, same lifecycle, QR-bill, reminder) with `parent_document_id` = the chantier's PROJECT document, its client/address/typed-in recipient/reference, the company's current VAT rate, the INVOICE template's texts and due days, the company's payment terms, **no early payment discount** (only ever on the final invoice), and one section + one line holding the same label - "Acompte n° 2 – 30 % selon offre OFF-..." in the client's language (`pdf-labels.ts`'s `depositLine`), n° counting every deposit of the chantier, a cancelled one included (a number is never printed twice), under the numbering lock - the "final invoice exists" check is under it too - excl. VAT computed back from the amount asked (`deposit.ts`'s `depositAmounts`, tested; the total incl. VAT can land a centime off on an odd amount). Its PDF is titled "FACTURE D'ACOMPTE", with no details page. `GET /project/:id/billing`: the quote's number and total, the deposits (archived included) and `summarizeBilling`'s invoiced (SENT/PAID - deposits, plus the final invoice's balance once there is one) / paid / remaining incl. VAT (0 once the final invoice is out). **The final invoice** (the chantier's one STANDARD invoice, "Facturer" as before) **deducts its deposits sent or paid** - unpaid ones too, the client owes them on their own invoices (`deposit.ts`'s `depositsToDeduct`/`amountDue`, tested): worked out from the chantier's deposits while it's a draft, frozen in `documents.deposit_deductions` (JSONB, `018_add_deposit_deductions.sql`, each deduction's `date` a plain `YYYY-MM-DD`) when it's sent or paid, never from a request - a status change of a chantier's invoice runs in one transaction with the chantier's PROJECT document locked `FOR UPDATE` (`updateDocumentStatusServ`), so cancelling a deposit and sending the final invoice deducting it never cross. `invoice_kind`/`deposit_deductions` are never taken from `POST /document` (always `STANDARD`), and a deposit has no "Dupliquer" (its copy would be a final invoice). It can't be created while a deposit is still a draft (409 `DEPOSIT_DRAFT_PENDING` - an archived draft doesn't count); a deposit it deducts, once sent, can't be cancelled (409 `DEPOSIT_DEDUCTED` - cancel the final invoice first), while it's a draft cancelling one just takes it off. `amount_incl_vat` stays the whole work's total; what's left to pay is computed: `GET /document/:id/deductions` (`{ deductions, amount_due }`, for the form), the PDF (titled "FACTURE FINALE" when it deducts any, "./. Acompte FAC-... du ..." rows then "Solde à payer" - or "Solde en votre faveur" - on the total's band, page 2 too), the QR-bill (the amount due - no slip at all when nothing is: `prepareQrBill` returns null), the early payment discount (on the amount due), the `{{montant}}` placeholder (intro/conclusion and the payment reminder) and the dashboard (`getOpenInvoicesFromDB`/`getPaidAmountByClientFromDB` subtract the frozen deductions - the deposits count on their own). `DocumentPdfGenerator`'s `PdfSource` carries `deductions`/`amountDue` for every PDF. **Demo data**: `019_add_demo_deposit_invoices.sql` gives company 2 (only - company 1 can be real data) two deposits of 30 % on "Attic insulation" (one paid, one sent) and a completed "Facade repainting" chantier with a paid deposit and its final invoice sent deducting it - a `DO` block, numbers continuing the company's own sequence, amounts hand-computed like the backend, frozen `billed_address`/`deposit_deductions`, doing nothing once company 2 has a deposit; `scripts/reset-demo-company.sql` ends with the same block (keep both in sync). `GET /project/:id/billing` also returns the chantier's `finalInvoice` and `deductible` (what a final invoice created now would deduct). **Frontend**: the chantier's page has a "Facturation" card (`features/projects/project-billing`): the quote, invoiced in deposits, received, left to invoice, its deposits (each opening its invoice), "Facture d'acompte" (a dialog: % of the quote incl. VAT - only with a quote - or an amount, the total shown before creating, a warning when the deposits would pass the quote, the new draft opened right away) until the final invoice exists, "Facture finale" once the chantier is completed (the same prefilled form "Facturer" opens), then a link to it. The documents list tags a deposit "Acompte" and a sent final invoice with deductions "Finale" (`display.ts`'s `invoiceKind`, tested - also searchable), the invoices list has Toutes / Acomptes / Finales tabs on that same rule (`kindFilter`), and it shows what's due (`display.ts`'s `invoiceAmountDue`, tested); the chantiers list's "Voir la facture" ignores deposits. The invoice form titles a deposit/final invoice so and lists a final invoice's deductions and its balance under the totals (`GET /document/:id/deductions` once saved, the chantier's `deductible` for a new one). Error codes with their own message: `api-error.ts`'s `httpErrorKey` maps `DEPOSIT_DRAFT_PENDING`/`DEPOSIT_DEDUCTED`/`DEPOSIT_NEEDS_QUOTE` to `notifications.codes.*`.
