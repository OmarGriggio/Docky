# PDFs and the Swiss QR-bill

The generators, the classic layout, styled text, the QR-bill (structured addresses, payment references, coded errors), the early payment discount and the standalone slip. The templates' look is in [templates.md](templates.md).

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-26 - keep it up to date in the same commit as the code it describes.

## Backend

- **PDFs** (`backend/src/pdf/`): invoice (with Swiss QR-bill), quote, payment reminder (with QR-bill). Template Method in `generators/document-pdf-generator.ts`; invoices and quotes laid out by `templates/business-letter.ts` (see the layout bullet below), the reminder too since 2026-11-18 (`reminder.template.ts`: the invoice's letterhead, header image, colors and footer, the reminder text in place of the positions - `letter.ts` only keeps the shared logo/header image/section details helpers), DTO building in `templates/dto/shared.ts`. Printed in the **client's** language (`clients.language`, not the user's UI one): every fixed string is in `templates/pdf-labels.ts` (`PDF_LABELS.FR`/`.EN`), DE/IT fall back to French. Totals printed are the stored `amount_excl_vat`/`amount_incl_vat` - never re-added in a template - and per-line/section totals use `document.calculations.ts`'s `lineTotal` (line discount included), so the PDF always matches the app. The QR-bill's `country` is mapped from free text to ISO (`swiss-qr-bill.dto.ts`'s table: Suisse/Switzerland/Schweiz/Svizzera...) - a country name missing from it makes the QR-bill fail.
- **Invoice and quote layout** (`pdf/templates/business-letter.ts`, shared by both - a change there applies to both): modelled on the company's own Excel invoices. Page 1: client number and place/date (`03.09.2024`) left, title + address right, intro, "FACTURE/OFFRE N° ..." underlined, "Réf." (`reference_client`), one line per section ("Pos 1"...) with its total, then the totals as a borderless table (Total / Rabais % / TVA N° % / **Total** between two rules / an invoice's early payment line), the conclusion, and a block right after it, in the right-hand column under the client's address (an invoice's payment conditions; a quote's validity + the company's payment terms, after its "Bon pour accord" lines). Page 2+: numbered sections ("Pos 1 – ..."), then the same totals. Amounts as `1'578.75` (`templates/format.ts`). The header image is on every page (`PdfWriter.setPageDecorator`) - at most the page's width between margins and 2.5 cm tall, never enlarged, the content starting the same distance below it whatever its height (an image under 1 cm keeps a 1 cm one's room) - the footer (company name/address/bank+IBAN left, website/email/phone right) on every page but the QR-bill's (`forEachNumberedPage` - the pages left out of numbering), and flowing text stops above it (`setBottomLimit`). **Colors, per element** (the template's palette and `element_colors` - see [templates.md](templates.md); a draft's read at print time, a sent document's frozen with its look; `element-colors.ts`'s `printColorsOf`, tested, gives `LetterDto.company.colors` one color or null per element): the body text (`PdfWriter.setTextColor` - every text drawn without a color of its own; `useStandardFont` puts black back for the QR-bill's page), the title and its underline, the modern header's company name, the sections' titles (details page and modern table), the modern table's header row, the total (a tinted band between rules of that color), the early payment line (a lighter band underlined in it), the conditions' heading, the payment scale's two rows, a quote's "Bon pour accord", the footer's rule, company name, website and email. An element without a color prints black (a band gray). Bands are always a tint of the color (`accent-color.ts`'s `tint`, tested), so black text stays readable. The Swiss QR-bill is never colored - its guidelines fix it black on white. The reminder uses the same letterhead, header image and footer, so the same colors. Every string drawn or measured goes through `PdfWriter`'s `printable`: what the font can't print (Helvetica only covers WinAnsi - an emoji typed in a field) becomes "?" instead of failing the whole PDF.
- **Styled text in a document's texts** (since 2026-11-19): an introduction, a conclusion and the reminder's text can style a passage with tags, like a placeholder - `[g]...[/g]` bold, `[c1]`...`[c5]` the text in the template's palette color of that position, `[f1]`...`[f5]` a band of it behind it - a 30 % tint, like the total's (`TEXT_BAND_TINT`; the preview's `color-mix`), the text keeping its own color unless also in a `[cN]`. `pdf/templates/rich-text.ts` (pure, tested - `parseRichText`, `richTextOf`, `layoutRichLines`): tags nest and are case-insensitive, a closing tag nothing opened prints as typed, an unclosed one runs to the end of the text, a color the palette doesn't have prints in the text color without a band - never a failed PDF. Parsed first, the `{{placeholders}}` filled run by run after (`richTextOf` - a value is never read as a tag), so `LetterDto.introduction`/`conclusion` and `ReminderDto.text` are `TextRun[]`, drawn by `PdfWriter.richText()` (line breaks like `text()`, each word measured in its own style, a band from below the descenders to above the capitals so two lines' bands touch) in both layouts. Frontend: `shared/utils/rich-text.ts` (the same parser, mirrored - keep both in sync - plus the toolbar's `wrapSelection`/`clearStyles`, tested), `shared/components/text-style-toolbar` above the textarea (bold, the two colors' text and band - one text and one band button per palette color, named after it - only bold without a palette - and an eraser taking the tags in and right around the selection off; it edits the textarea itself then fires `input`, like `TabIndentDirective`, so ngModel and reactive forms alike) on the document form's `document-text-block` and the `/settings/templates` page, and `shared/components/rich-text-view` showing the styled text in the form's previews (plain bindings, never `innerHTML`).
- **QR-bill addresses are structured (type `S`)** since 2026-11-13 - the combined type `K` the QR-bill used to send isn't accepted since the guidelines' version 2.3 (22.11.2025), and banks stop processing it around mid-November 2026. Name, street, building number, postcode, town and country each in its own field (`swiss-qr-bill.types.ts`, payload in `swiss-qr-bill.payload.ts`, tested): the street keeps its number as typed ("Rue de Lausanne 12", tolerated by the guidelines - no column splits them), the building number is left empty. The QR code itself is error correction level `M` (the guidelines') without a margin of its own, so it fills its 46 × 46 mm lined up with the payment part's text, in the PDF (`swiss-qr-bill.generator.ts`) like in the slip's SVG - and the PDF's payment part (`swiss-qr-bill.template.ts`) uses the SVG's own spacing (content 7 mm from the receipt's line, the text column 8 mm from the QR code, the guidelines' blank-field sizes). The payment part prints the additional information the QR code carries (an invoice's "Facture FAC-..."), not the receipt - like the guidelines say. A complement/c/o isn't in the QR-bill's address ("irrelevant for the payer", the guidelines say) - it stays on the document's address. A payer the structured address can't hold - no postcode or town yet, a town past 35 characters, a postcode past 16, a country that isn't a code - gets the blank "Payable by" box instead, never a 422 (`completeDebtorOrNull`, tested - a sent invoice's frozen address can't be fixed); no country means Switzerland. The creditor needs the company's postcode and town, not its street - the profile holds them to the QR-bill's 16 and 35 characters.
- **Payment references on QR-bills** (since 2026-11-13, `020_add_payment_reference.sql`, `pdf/swiss-qr-bill/payment-reference.ts`, tested against the guidelines' and ISO 11649's own examples): a company setting, not per invoice - an optional **QR-IBAN** (`companies.qr_iban`, checked to be one: CH/LI, institution id 30000-31999, valid modulo 97 - a 400 `QR_IBAN_INVALID` otherwise) makes every invoice's QR-bill a **QR reference** (QRR, 27 digits, recursive modulo 10 - the invoice number's digits, `FAC-2026-0012` → `...20260012` + check digit) paid to that QR-IBAN (a QR-IBAN requires one, and only it does - the validator checks the pair); without one, `companies.payment_reference_type` picks a **creditor reference** (`SCOR`, the default: "RF" + modulo 97 check digits + `FAC20260012`) or none (`NONE`). The ordinary IBAN field refuses a QR-IBAN (a 400 `IBAN_IS_QR_IBAN`, its own message on the profile): an invoice sent with a creditor reference or none can only be paid to an ordinary IBAN - with only a QR-IBAN there, its PDF would fail. One stored there before this still counts as the QR-IBAN (`qrIbanOf`). A draft invoice's reference follows the company's settings; **it's frozen on the invoice when it leaves DRAFT** (`documents.payment_reference`, `updateDocumentStatusInDB`, never rewritten, never from a request - and only then: one sent without a reference keeps none when it's paid or cancelled later) - an invoice sent before this has none and keeps none. A frozen QR reference whose company has since dropped its QR-IBAN prints without a reference, to the ordinary IBAN. Printed under "Référence" on the receipt and the payment part (QRR in blocks of 5 after 2 digits, SCOR in blocks of 4). The standalone slip never has one (no document to match). The footer keeps printing the ordinary IBAN. What it's for: matching a bank file's payments to their invoices (not built yet).
- **QR-bill errors are a coded 422**, not a 500: `SwissQrBillValidationError` (an `AppError`) carries `COMPANY_IBAN_MISSING`/`COMPANY_IBAN_INVALID`/`COMPANY_ADDRESS_INCOMPLETE`/`QR_BILL_AMOUNT_INVALID`/`QR_BILL_INVALID` as `code` in the JSON body (`AppError`'s optional third argument, see `shared/types/errors.ts`), which the frontend maps to a translated `qrBillErrors.*` message (`shared/utils/api-error.ts` - a PDF error body arrives as a Blob). The company IBAN is stripped of spaces first.
- **Early payment discount ("escompte")** (`010_add_early_payment_discount.sql`, on the template since 025): rate %, days and a minimum total incl. VAT on the invoice's template, copied onto an invoice when it's created (`addDocumentServ`, never from the request body - and again when a draft's template is changed) and frozen there like `vat_rate` - not editable per invoice yet. `document.calculations.ts`'s `computeEarlyPayment` (rate% of the total incl. VAT, to the cent - not rounded to 5 centimes, on purpose for now). Printed only; the QR-bill always asks for the full amount. Under an invoice's payment conditions, a small scale (`pdf/templates/payment-scale.ts`'s `buildPaymentScale`, tested): below the minimum net, from it the rate within its days (on a band of the accent color), net within the due date's days (on one of the secondary color; light gray without colors) - only what applies to that invoice highlighted (under the minimum, the "below" line, never the discount) - nothing without an early payment discount. The footer's website/bank name/bank account are `009_add_company_footer_details.sql`.
- **Standalone QR-bill slip** (`/qr-bill` page, `POST /pdf/qr-bill-slip`, `pdf/qr-bill-slip.ts`): a bare payment slip for a payer typed in or picked from the clients, optional amount, no document behind it - rendered as an SVG at its official 210 × 105 mm (`pdf/templates/swiss-qr-bill.svg.ts`, the guidelines' own font sizes and blank-field sizes, framed by a thin line). The page turns it into a 300 dpi PNG and copies it to the clipboard as both `image/png` and an HTML `<img>` sized in mm, so Word/Excel paste it at its real size. A line too wide for the receipt column (measured with pdf-lib's Helvetica metrics) is a 400. Name, postcode and town required for a payer. Its "À l'attention de" (a contact person, prefilled from the client's address) goes in the QR-bill's additional information ("À l'attention de ..." / "Attention: ...", `pdf-labels.ts`'s `qrAttention`), printed in the payment part - the structured address has no field for it; a 400 if it doesn't fit that column's one line.
