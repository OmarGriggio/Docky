# Documents - quotes, invoices, chantiers

How a quote, an invoice and a chantier's PROJECT document live: statuses and what stays editable, the frozen address, the typed-in recipient, saving content, sections, rounding, VAT, the service period, default texts, and the chantier's own screens.

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-26 - keep it up to date in the same commit as the code it describes.

## Backend

- **Chantiers list ordered by urgency**: `project.repository.ts`'s `getProjectsFromDB` sorts `IN_PROGRESS` projects by the `date_start` (among that chantier's own sections) closest to right now - either direction, an overdue section is just as worth surfacing as an upcoming one - via a `LEFT JOIN LATERAL` picking the single closest one per project (a plain `MIN()` can't express "closest to now", only "earliest overall"); `COMPLETED` projects always sort last regardless of their own dates. Exposed as `closest_section_date` on the list response.
- **A chantier is a `PROJECT` document**: every `projects` row owns exactly one `documents` row of type `PROJECT` (`projects.document_id`), whose sections/lines are the chantier's resource ledger (`project_resources` is gone). Accepting a quote (`acceptQuoteServ`) creates both and copies every line of the quote into it (typed by hand ones included - the final invoice is built from that ledger; not a line's own discount, the ledger has none; a section without any line is skipped), and answers the quote with the new chantier's `project_id` - the frontend opens that chantier straight away, from the documents list or the document form; a hand-created chantier starts empty. `document-form.ts`'s "Charger chantier" prefills an invoice from that ledger - every line of it, even a resource already on a section typed by hand (the catalog picker only keeps a resource out of its own section).
- **Document lifecycle, enforced server-side** (the frontend locks the same cases, but this is the real rule):
  - Statuses: a quote `DRAFT`/`SENT` → `ACCEPTED` (only via `POST /document/:id/accept`, creates the chantier) or `REJECTED`; an invoice `DRAFT` → `SENT` → `PAID` or `CANCELLED`, forward only (a `SENT` invoice never goes back to `DRAFT`). Allowed transitions per current status: `SETTABLE_STATUSES`, mirrored once on the frontend by `shared/utils/document-status.ts`'s `nextStatuses` (tested - plus ACCEPTED for a quote, nothing for an archived document), read by both the list and the form. Changed from the documents list's Statut cell or from the document form's own status bar (`document-form.ts`'s `nextStatuses`/`requestStatus`: a reachable step is a button, plus "Refuser l'offre"/"Annuler la facture"; refused while the form has unsaved changes, confirmed unless it's a quote's DRAFT <-> SENT, the page reloaded after). Lists (`GET /document`) are newest first (`ORDER BY id DESC` - creation order). `POST /document` always creates an active `DRAFT`, whatever the body says. `discount`/`vat_rate` must be 0-100.
  - Header and content (sections/lines) are editable only while a quote is `DRAFT`/`SENT`, an invoice is `DRAFT` (`isOpenForEditing` - once sent, the client holds it under its number: a wrong one is cancelled and duplicated, not rewritten), or a `PROJECT`'s chantier is `IN_PROGRESS` - `assertContentEditableServ`, checked by every line/section write. A section's dates and calendar note stay editable (planning, not content).
  - A `COMPLETED` chantier is invoiced once: a second `POST /document` with the same `parent_document_id` is a 409, unless the earlier invoice was cancelled (an archived one still counts - archiving tidies a list, it doesn't undo an invoice). The project list shows "Voir la facture" instead of "Facturer" then. Its **deposit invoices** don't count as that invoice (`getInvoiceForProjectDocumentFromDB` only looks at `invoice_kind = 'STANDARD'`).
- A status change (`updateDocumentStatusInDB`) also requires `DRAFT`/`SENT` and an active document in the `UPDATE` itself, so it can't overwrite a concurrent accept.
  - **The address a document is sent to is frozen** (`documents.billed_address` JSONB, `016_add_document_billed_address.sql`, which also froze every document already past DRAFT): once a quote/invoice leaves DRAFT (a status change or an accept), its client's name, billing address and Lieu/Bâtiment are copied onto it (`billed-address.ts`'s `buildBilledAddress`, tested) and the PDFs and QR-bill print that from then on (`billedAddressOf`) - a client who moves or is renamed later no longer changes an invoice already sent, like `vat_rate` and the early payment discount. A quote back to DRAFT follows its client again; a SENT quote edited is frozen afresh (only while still SENT - a concurrent accept or back-to-DRAFT wins). Null for a draft, for a typed-in recipient (already on the document) and for a client without any address yet (frozen empty, it could never be fixed; the next status change freezes it). The document form shows the frozen address instead of the client's current one, marked as such. The weekly demo reset freezes company 2's sent documents the same way. Never written from a request. The payment reminder is the exception: a new letter, it and its QR-bill go to the client's current address (`withCurrentAddress`) - a client may have moved since the invoice was sent.
  - Accepting a quote flips its status first, inside the transaction, with the `DRAFT`/`SENT` check in the `UPDATE` itself (`acceptDocumentInDB`) - a concurrent second accept matches nothing, so one quote never spawns two chantiers.
- **A document without a client from the list ("Saisie manuelle")**: every company has exactly one **"Non défini"** client (`clients.is_placeholder`, `007_add_placeholder_client.sql`, created with the company by `company.service.ts`'s `addCompanyServ`) - protected (no edit, archive or address: 409), hidden from the clients list and every client picker. A quote/invoice made out to it carries its recipient typed by hand on the document itself (`recipient_name`/`_complement`/`_street`/`_postal_code`/`_city`, `008_add_document_recipient_columns.sql`; name + postcode + town required, no control characters, each field within the QR-bill's own length (name/street 70, postcode 16, town 35) - `document.service.ts`'s `resolveRecipient`; always null for a real client). PDFs, the QR-bill and the reminder print that recipient instead of the client (`pdf/templates/dto/shared.ts`'s `buildClientDto`); greeting "Madame, Monsieur", language FR and country Switzerland come from the placeholder. Accepting such a quote carries the recipient onto its chantier, named after it. Company 2's (the English demo's) placeholder is "Not specified" / "Dear Sir or Madam" / EN instead - set by 007 and kept that way by the weekly demo reset; the UI shows the translated `clients.placeholderName` wherever a client name would be (dashboard, chantiers), and a chantier's detail and Lieu column show its typed-in recipient. `documents.client_id` stays `NOT NULL` - see [zz_docs/Decisions.md](../Decisions.md) for why this over a nullable client or one auto-created client per document.
- **Saving a document's content**: `PUT /document/:id/content` takes the whole draft (`{ sections: [{ title, description, date_start, date_end, note, lines: [...] }] }`), archives every current section/line and recreates them in one transaction, then recomputes totals once. Both `document-form.ts` and `project-resources.ts` save through it (`DocumentService.replaceContent`) - never one request per line again. Line types, labels, quantities, prices, a line's own discount (0-100), section titles, a 500-line cap and a line's `resource_id` (must be the company's own) are validated there; `document_lines.type` has no CHECK constraint of its own.
- **Documents have real sections**: `document_sections` is a grouping title every `document_lines` row belongs to via a required `section_id` (replaces an earlier flat-list-with-marker-line approach — reopened, see [zz_docs/Decisions.md](../Decisions.md)). `document_lines.type` is `MATERIAL`/`SERVICE` only, both always priced (`quantity`/`unit_price` `NOT NULL`), both using `quantity`+`unit` for their amount (e.g. `"5 Heure"` for a service — no separate time field). `position` is scoped to the line's section; `document_sections.position` orders sections. `addLineServ` checks the given `section_id` belongs to the target document.
- **Rounding**: each line is rounded to the cent first (`document.calculations.ts`'s `lineTotal`, half up via `round2` with `Number.EPSILON`), and `amount_excl_vat` is the sum of those rounded lines with the document discount applied - the same rule as the form (`document-draft.ts`) and the PDF, so lines, section subtotals and the stored total always add up exactly.
- **VAT**: `documents.vat_rate` (one rate per document — Switzerland has a single standard rate) applied on `amount_excl_vat` by `computeDocumentTotals`. **Payment terms**: `payment_terms` (free text) + `due_date` (structured), set directly, no computation. **Service date or period** (art. 26 LTVA, since 2026-11-26, `036_add_invoice_service_period.sql`): an invoice's `service_date_start` (a single date) and `service_date_end` (a period) - `service-period.ts`'s `normalizeServicePeriod` (tested: an end alone becomes the single date, an end on the start day none, an end before the start a 400 `SERVICE_PERIOD_INVALID`, anything but `YYYY-MM-DD` a 400 `SERVICE_DATE_INVALID`, always null on a quote), editable while a draft like the rest of the header, kept when `PUT /document/:id` leaves both out. Printed under the reference ("Période de la prestation : 02.11.2026 – 13.11.2026" / "Date de la prestation", the modern layout's right-hand column "Prestation" - `format.ts`'s `servicePeriodOf`, tested), nothing without one. The form's "Prestation du / au" (under the client reference) is prefilled from the chantier's planned sections when its ledger is loaded ("Facturer", "Charger chantier" - `shared/utils/service-period.ts`'s `servicePeriodOfSections`, tested), unless already set; a duplicate keeps its source's. Not required - an invoice without one prints none.
- **A document's default texts** (introduction, conclusion, due days, payment terms) come from its template (see [templates.md](templates.md)) - purely client-side for a quote/invoice: `document-form.ts` fills a new document's fields from it, the document holds its own copy - an invoice's payment terms included, edited on the form like its introduction and conclusion (`document-text-block`'s `paymentTerms` field: plain text, no styles or variables - the PDF prints them as typed; "Sauvegarder" writes the template's `payment_terms`), a template changed later never rewrites them; a quote has no field, it prints its template's. The reminder's text is the exception, read from the invoice's template every time its PDF is printed.

## Frontend

- **A chantier's resources open read-only** (`project-resources.ts`), an IN_PROGRESS one included: "Modifier" switches to editing, "Annuler" reloads what's stored, "Enregistrer" saves and goes back to reading. A COMPLETED one has no "Modifier" at all (`locked`). A line comes from the catalog or is typed by hand ("Ajouter une ligne" - its type picked on the line, `resource_id` null), like a quote's. The chantier's page (`project-detail`) closes it too ("Clôturer", same confirmation as the list - disabled while its resources are being edited, it would close on what's stored, not on the unsaved lines on screen).
